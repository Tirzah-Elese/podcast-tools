My Values Hand

Tools, jobs and technology will keep changing, and some of the skills you'll need haven't been invented yet. What you value is what you carry with you through all of it. Hold up your hand: five fingers, five values. Write them down, then take a few minutes to ask where each one came from and how it shows up in your life.

Write a value on each finger

Type one value beside each finger. A single word or a short phrase works best, like honesty, family, courage or being curious.

Look closer at each value

Open each finger and answer as honestly as you can. There are no right answers. Short notes are fine.

Keep your hand

Your answers stay in this browser on this device, so you can come back to this page and pick up where you left off. To keep a copy somewhere else, copy your reflection and paste it into your notes, a journal or an email to yourself.

A reflection exercise from ExelAcademy, shared on our podcast. Come back to it in a few months and see which values have held.
